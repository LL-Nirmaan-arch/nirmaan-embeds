Celebrating India's Rich Linguistic Diversity

India's extraordinary linguistic diversity is one of its greatest strengths. When states and union territories were reorganized, language became a defining factor, and each state adopted its predominant language as its official language. Today, the Constitution of India recognizes 22 Scheduled Languages.
